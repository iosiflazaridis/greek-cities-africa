# Greek Cities in Present-Day Africa

An interactive map, statistics and the full list of the cities whose site lies in present-day Africa (Egypt with Sinai, Libya, Algeria and Sudan) and that became Greek,
or were bilingual with many Greek speakers beside their own language, between the first Greek settlements in Cyrenaica (c. 637 BC) and the Arab conquest
(Egypt 641/642, Cyrenaica 642/643), with when the last Greek speakers were gone from each; and the Greek Orthodox Patriarchate of Alexandria, which outlived the cities.

**Open the page:** https://iosiflazaridis.github.io/greek-cities-africa/

- `index.html`: the page (self-contained; the data are embedded)
- `img/`: the ten images in full size; `thumb/`: their thumbnails
- stories from 17 of the cities (their scholars, poets and philosophers, and events) are part of the page and of the data files
- `dl/`: the data as CSV, JSON and an Excel workbook (with the places left out, with reasons, the year-by-year series, the sees of the Patriarchate and the Greek communities after 1800)

Sources: the Inventory of Archaic and Classical Poleis (Hansen & Nielsen 2004), the ancient authors, papyri and inscriptions, Hierocles, Worp's checklist of the bishops of Egypt (1994),
the Pleiades gazetteer; for the Patriarchate and the modern Greeks, the Patriarchate of Alexandria, Dalachanis (2017), Hamilton (2006) and the Greek Foreign Ministry;
ancient DNA, Ringbauer et al. (Nature 2025) and Moots et al. (Nature Ecology and Evolution 2023). Every record in the data keeps its own sources and notes. Map: Natural Earth.
